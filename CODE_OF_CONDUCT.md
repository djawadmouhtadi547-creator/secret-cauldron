# Community Code of Conduct

We want the Prime Launcher community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback and discuss code, not personal traits.
- Welcome different experience levels and language backgrounds.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's logs, identifiers, or account details.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove violating content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. This document is a lightweight project policy and may be expanded as the community grows.
